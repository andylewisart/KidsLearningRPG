# Style bible

Every image in the game comes from ChatGPT, so consistency comes from pasting the same words every time. This file holds those words.

## The look in one line

**Cinematic techno-fantasy.** Crystals, airships, armor and machines, plus monsters the size of skyscrapers. It's epic and intense like a big console RPG or a giant-monster movie. Never cute or babyish, and always kid-safe (no blood or gore).

### Style A: Cinematic

```text
Art style: cinematic concept art for a big-budget fantasy and science-fiction video game. Detailed digital painting with realistic materials: scratched steel, worn leather, wet scales, glowing crystal. Dramatic lighting: a cool key light from the upper left, a warm orange rim light, deep shadows, light atmospheric haze and drifting sparks. Epic, intense and grown-up in tone, like a premium console RPG. Never cartoonish or cute.
```

### Style B: Full-art card

```text
Art style: premium full-art collectible-card illustration. Bold, clean linework with crisp cel shading and painterly highlights. Vivid elemental colors (electric blue, molten orange, toxic green, royal violet) against darker backgrounds. Dynamic poses, dramatic perspective and swirling energy effects. Powerful and intense, anime-influenced but mature. Never babyish or chibi.
```

### Style C: Pixel diorama

```text
Art style: high-detail 2D pixel art, like a modern remaster of a classic 16-bit role-playing game. Crisp pixel-perfect characters with a limited palette, dark outlines and dithered shading, placed in a richly detailed diorama-like world with soft depth of field, bloom and glowing light effects. Epic and atmospheric. Never cute.
```

### Style D: Ink and watercolor

```text
Art style: elegant ink-and-watercolor fantasy illustration, like the concept paintings of classic Japanese role-playing games. Flowing, calligraphic ink linework, ornate armor with fine filigree, washes of deep indigo, crimson and gold, soft bleeding edges and visible paper texture. Mysterious, dreamlike and heroic.
```

## Rules block (paste with the style in every batch)

```text
Rules for every image in this chat:
- Completely original designs. Never imitate an existing franchise, character, creature, logo or emblem.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, signatures, borders, frames or user interface, unless a prompt asks for glowing magic runes.
- Epic, fierce and intense is great (roaring monsters, glowing eyes, battle-ready poses), but no blood, gore, wounds or cruelty.
- Keep the art style identical across every image in this chat.
```

## Writing new prompts

- **Never name a franchise or famous character** in a prompt (no Final Fantasy, Pokémon, Godzilla, Star Wars, and so on). Story Quest already found that OpenAI blocks lookalikes, such as a dark-scaled creature with glowing back spikes. Describe what you like about it instead ("a colossal sea titan with a wall of jagged dorsal crystals"), and add one twist nobody has seen.
- **Characters and creatures:** full body with nothing cut off, centered with empty space around it, three-quarter view, lit from the upper left, transparent background.
- **Battle backgrounds:** no characters, wide landscape, and the lower third kept fairly open (that's where the fighters stand).
- **Don't worry about facing left or right.** The game mirrors images as needed.
- **Asset names** say what each image is for: `hero_`, `ally_`, `tutor_`, `enemy_`, `boss_`, `titan_`, `bg_`, `story_`, `icon_`.
