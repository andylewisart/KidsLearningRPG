# Batch 00: Style audition

**Goal:** let him pick the art style for the whole game.
**Images:** 4 (one per style), plus an optional fifth if he wants a mix.
**Time:** about 10–15 minutes. Do this one with him.

Every prompt paints the same battle scene, so the only difference is the style. The two heroes, the spellcaster and the titan in it are drafts, and he gets to redesign the cast in batch 01.

## How to run it

1. **Use a new ChatGPT chat for each style** (4 chats). If they share a chat, the styles bleed into each other.
2. Paste the whole prompt block, exactly as written.
3. If an image comes out cut off or has text in it, reply "Same scene, but show all three heroes and the whole titan, and no text."
4. Save the images as `audition_A.png`, `audition_B.png`, `audition_C.png` and `audition_D.png`.

## Prompt A: Cinematic

```text
Art style: cinematic concept art for a big-budget fantasy and science-fiction video game. Detailed digital painting with realistic materials: scratched steel, worn leather, wet scales, glowing crystal. Dramatic lighting: a cool key light from the upper left, a warm orange rim light, deep shadows, light atmospheric haze and drifting sparks. Epic, intense and grown-up in tone, like a premium console RPG. Never cartoonish or cute.

Paint a wide landscape (3:2) image: a side-view battle scene from a turn-based role-playing game, like a gameplay screenshot but with no user interface or text on screen.

On the right side, three heroes stand in a loose row, facing left toward the enemy:
1. a teenage crystal knight in sleek dark-steel armor with glowing blue crystal inlays, holding a greatsword whose edge glows like blue plasma;
2. a young sky-pirate sharpshooter in a long patched flight coat, goggles pushed up on their head, aiming two crystal-powered blaster pistols that glow orange;
3. a hooded spellcaster whose face is hidden in shadow except for two glowing golden eyes, with glowing rune-letters orbiting their raised hands.

On the left, towering over them, a colossal titan: a four-legged beast with a hammerhead-shaped skull, six glowing amber eyes, and armor plates made of cracked geode crystal glowing violet from within, steam venting from the cracks. Its tail ends in a huge crystal club.

Setting: a canyon of giant glowing crystals at dusk, with a wrecked airship half-buried in the cliffs behind them.

Completely original designs (no existing franchises or characters). Intense but kid-safe: no blood or gore. No text, logos or watermarks.
```

## Optional prompt E: his mix

If he likes parts of two styles ("A's monster but B's heroes"), open the chat with the style he liked best, attach the other image, and paste:

```text
Paint this same battle scene again in your current style, but borrow these things from the attached image: (describe what he liked, in his words).
```

Save it as `audition_E.png`.

## Pick the winner

Put the images side by side and ask him:

- Which one would you most want to play? Why?
- What would you change about it?
- Does the titan look scary enough? Cool enough?

Write down what he says, in his words. Paste it into your message to Claude along with the upload. His reasons decide the tweaks to the style block, like "brighter," "more armor" or "bigger monster."

## Upload

Put the images in `art/inbox/batch-00/` and tell Claude which one won (and why). Claude will:

1. copy the winning style into [`../style-bible.md`](../style-bible.md) under **Current style**, with his tweaks, and
2. fill in the setup message of batch 01 so you can paste it as-is.
