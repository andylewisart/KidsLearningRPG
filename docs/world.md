# The Sundered Isles: world bible

The setting is a real place with its own history, not a simulation. Every line of dialogue, item description and joke should fit here. Tone: sun, sea, crystals and ruins, the art of Final Fantasy X and the jokes of Monkey Island. Kid-safe and never babyish.

## The world in one paragraph

The **Sundered Isles** are a hundred tropical islands scattered across the **Glimmering Sea**. Each one has a **Lore Crystal**, a giant glowing crystal that holds what its island knows: every word, every number, every story, every song. The crystals light the harbors, lift the airships and keep the island's memory. When a crystal dims, its island **forgets**, and fiends crawl out of the dark that the forgetting leaves behind.

## The Forgetting

This is why math, spelling and writing matter in the isles. (The parent approved it on 12 October; `docs/story/chapter1-proposal.md`, section 2.)

- **An island remembers what its crystal knows.** As a crystal dims, its island forgets, newest things first: how to count change, read a tide chart and spell its own street names; later its recipes, its stories and the words to its songs. People can tell something's missing, but not what. Nobody forgets the people they love; they lose the words for things.
- **Nothing forgotten is gone.** It waits in the dark, and a lit crystal brings it back. What someone knows by heart, the dark takes last of all.
- **A new crystal learns from whoever carries it.** Every true thing worked out near it (a subtraction, a word spelled right, a sentence that paints a picture) is light it keeps. Guesses light nothing. The brighter it shines, the more its island remembers. That's why the Order sends a knight to carry each new crystal and a tutor droid to keep it learning on the way, and why every right answer he gives is something Driftwood gets back.
- **Fiends** are what the dark makes of forgotten things. When one bursts into glowing motes, that's what it swallowed, going home.
- **Jumble's scrambled signs** are his own mischief, not the crystal's: an island that's forgetting can't unscramble its own signs, which is exactly why he bothers.

## History

- **Long ago: Lumeria.** The isles were once one great land, Lumeria, home of the **Sages**. They grew the first Lore Crystals, and wrote every word they knew into them.
- **The Sundering (about 1,000 years ago).** The **Leviathan**, a sea titan longer than a mountain range, rose from the deep and struck Lumeria. The land cracked into a hundred islands.
- **The first Titan Callers** bound the first **Titans**, colossal creatures of sea, stone and storm, and drove the Leviathan back into the deep. Ever since, a Titan Caller has stood watch in every age.
- **The crystals scattered.** The Sages planted a Lore Crystal on each island so knowledge could never be lost all at once again.
- **The Order of the Crystal Knights** was founded to guard the crystals and carry new ones where they're needed. Its knights follow the **Crystal Code**: four hundred very serious rules. They include "Never leave a crystal behind" (rule eleven) and "Always finish your vegetables" (rule twelve).
- **Today.** Pirate ports, trading towns, jungle temples and crystal-powered airships. The Leviathan stirs again: it rises every few years and levels a city. The crystals are dimming faster than anyone can explain, and whole islands are forgetting.

## Who's causing trouble

- **Captain Jumble**, a ghost-pirate sorcerer:
  - He failed the Sage exams three hundred years ago, by one silent letter: he spelled KNIGHT without its K. Since then he has sailed his ghost galleon, the *Misspelled*, around the isles scrambling words and numbers and wrecking crystals.
  - He wants the isles to forget: in a world that forgets, nobody remembers who failed the Sage exams. Driftwood's forgetting is his project.
  - He is loud, vain, theatrical and secretly insecure. He loves anagrams, hates being corrected, and says silent letters out loud on purpose ("a kuh-NIGHT").
  - His final defeat will be a Wit Duel.
- **The Iron Dominion**, an empire of masked commanders and drone fleets:
  - It wants to drain the Lore Crystals to control the Leviathan, and a drained island forgets fast.
  - Its **scavenger drones** sniff out crystal shards wherever trouble breaks one.
- **Fiends** are what the dark makes of forgotten things: Scrap Raptors, Volt Jellies, Magnet Beetles, Ink Slimes and worse. They burst into glowing motes when beaten, and what they swallowed goes home.

## The party

Their voices and personalities are in `docs/game-design.md`.

- **Cade, the Crystal Knight:**
  - The newest knight of the Order, on his first real mission: carrying the new crystal to Driftwood Isle. He nearly failed his knight's test (he forgot rule twelve) and came back the next day to say all four hundred.
  - Earnest, formal and protective, and a terrible joke-teller. Quotes the Crystal Code constantly. No helmet.
  - Fights with subtraction.
- **Kit:**
  - The Order's tutor droid, assigned to Cade. It taught him through his knight training, and was teaching the new crystal its times tables when Jumble struck.
  - Deadpan, dry and secretly fond of him. Kit's tiny pirate hat was won in a card game, and Kit insists it is intimidating. A good tutor works itself out of a job, and Kit knows it.
  - Helps when the player is stuck.
- **Captain Wren, the Sky-Pirate Gunner:**
  - Captain of the airship *Brass Albatross*, hired to fly Cade and the new crystal to Driftwood Isle. The Albatross was her grandma's ship, then her mom's, and Wren is the first one who ever crashed her.
  - Cocky, fast-talking, gives everything nicknames, and secretly the team's biggest cheerleader.
  - Fights with multiplication and division.
- **Knox, the Spellwright:**
  - A tiny, three-hundred-year-old scholar from the last Sage school. The K in Knox is silent, which is the Spellwright's favorite thing about it. Captain Jumble was his study partner: Knox passed the exam Jumble failed, Jumble never spoke to him again, and Knox still saves his seat in the library.
  - Speaks like an ancient wizard, then gets thrilled about silent letters.
  - Casts spells by spelling.
- **Maren, the Titan Caller:**
  - Keeper of the sea shrines. Her name means "of the sea". The Lore Crystals send her dreams when a Titan is needed.
  - Her island's crystal went dark when she was little, and its people forgot their song. She knew it by heart, so she sings it to the sea every day: the sea remembers what people forget. One day she'll bring it home.
  - Calm, wise, poetic, and grand when she summons.
  - Calls Titans with writing.

## Driftwood Isle (Chapter 1)

A small island of white beaches, jungle temples and a canyon of giant crystals. Its harbor town, **Driftwood Harbor**, is a pirate port built from shipwrecks. Its Lore Crystal, in the harbor's lighthouse, is nearly out, and the island has started forgetting.

**The opening: the crash (night into dawn).**
1. Just before dawn, the *Brass Albatross* is carrying the new Lore Crystal to Driftwood Isle. On deck, Kit is teaching it, and his own answer makes it glow. Cade has been reading it the Crystal Code (it did not glow for rule twelve). Wren says nothing catches the Albatross. Pockets has stowed away in the rigging.
2. Captain Jumble's ghost galleon bursts out of a green cloud. He wants Driftwood left forgetting, Kit corrects his "kuh-NIGHT", and nobody corrects Captain Jumble: he rams them.
3. The crystal breaks into **four shards**. Wren catches a rope and rides her ship down into the **Crystal Canyon**, and Jumble flies off.
4. At dawn, the shards streak down all over the island. The knight comes down somewhere softer: the beach at **Shipwreck Cove**. At sunrise, Kit, its hover dented, drops out of the sky onto him.
5. The crash wakes the **Geode Titan**, a colossal crystal-eating beast that has slept under the canyon since the Sundering.

**Places:**

| Place | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Shipwreck Cove** | Where the story starts, at sunrise. The hulk of a very old shipwreck (not theirs: "Ours is worse"), tide pools and a signpost Jumble scrambled. A shard fell through a crack into an old chest with a number lock. Scrap Raptors and Magnet Beetles scavenge the sand. |
| **The Temple Ruins** | A Sage temple swallowed by jungle, with glowing glyphs on the walls. Its great round stone door is sealed with a number lock. Ink Slimes ooze around the steps. |
| **The Hall of Glyphs** | Inside the temple: a dim hall of carved pillars, light falling from cracks in the roof, shelves of stone tablets (the Sage library) and a mural of the old school, with one scholar missing from it. Jumble has trapped the Spellwright here in a cage of scrambled words. |
| **The Crystal Canyon** | Where the *Brass Albatross* came down. The Gunner is trying to repair her blasters. A cliff path down to the sea is blocked by fallen crystal boulders, and the Geode Titan's lair is a cave mouth rimmed with crystal teeth. Dominion drones and Volt Jellies buzz around the wreck. |
| **The Tide Grotto** | A sea cave below the canyon, glowing turquoise, with tide pools and a sea shrine. The Titan Caller keeps the shrine, sings her island's song to the tide, and waits for someone who can wake it. Volt Jellies drift in with the tide. |
| **Driftwood Harbor** (chapter 2) | The pirate port: Honest Hal's Previously Plundered Goods, the Salty Biscuit café (no grog, very good biscuits), the Sword Master's dock and the lighthouse with the island's Lore Crystal. It has gone quiet: no bells, no singing. The town is forgetting its songs and its sums (Honest Hal blames the dimming for his wrong totals, which only ever go wrong in his favor). |

Every place has a **rest crystal**, a glowing crystal on a stone base where the party can rest and heal.

**The island map.** He travels between places on a painted map of Driftwood Isle: walking off the edge of a scene opens it, and a tiny party walks the trails to wherever he clicks. Trails open as the story goes: the fixed signpost shows the way to the temple, Knox reads open the Sage gate to the canyon, and the party zips across the canyon's chasm to reach the Tide Grotto. Places for later chapters are on the map as teasers he can see but not visit yet: **Driftwood Harbor** (chapter 2), **Smoke Mountain** (a smoking volcano), **the Monkey Head** (a giant stone three-eyed monkey carved into a cliff: Pockets' ancestors?), **the Sage observatory** on a sea stack, and an **Iron Dominion watchtower** on the eastern cape.

**Running jokes on Driftwood Isle:**
- **Pockets, the three-eyed monkey,** stowed away on the Albatross, turns up in every scene, and finding him is a collectible. He steals shiny things, loves squeaky ones, and wears a tricorn just like Kit's. Captain Wren named him: everything shiny goes in his pockets, and he doesn't have pockets. At the end of chapter 1 he steals Kit's hat, to make a matching set.
- **Jumble's scrambled signs:** "PELMET" for TEMPLE, "NYCOAN" for CANYON.
- **The silent K:** Jumble's "kuh-NIGHT", Knox's favorite letter, and Cade's battle cry.
- **The Crystal Code:** rule eleven (never leave a crystal behind), rule twelve (always finish your vegetables, the one Cade forgot on his knight's test), and rule four hundred and one, which Cade adds himself: never do it alone.
- **Kit's hat**, and the Knight's attempts at jokes.
