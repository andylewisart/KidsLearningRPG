# The Sundered Isles: world bible

The setting is a real place with its own history, not a simulation. Every line of dialogue, item description and joke should fit here. Tone: sun, sea, crystals and ruins, the art of Final Fantasy X and the jokes of Monkey Island. Kid-safe and never babyish.

## The world in one paragraph

The **Sundered Isles** are a hundred tropical islands scattered across the **Glimmering Sea**. Each one has a **Lore Crystal**, a giant glowing crystal that remembers everything people know: every word, every number, every story. The crystals light the harbors, lift the airships and keep the island's memory. When a crystal dims, the island starts to forget. Letters fall off signs, numbers scramble on shop tags, and fiends crawl out of the shadows that knowledge leaves behind.

## History

- **Long ago: Lumeria.** The isles were once one great land, Lumeria, home of the **Sages**. They grew the first Lore Crystals, and wrote every word they knew into them.
- **The Sundering (about 1,000 years ago).** The **Leviathan**, a sea titan longer than a mountain range, rose from the deep and struck Lumeria. The land cracked into a hundred islands.
- **The first Titan Callers** bound the first **Titans**, colossal creatures of sea, stone and storm, and drove the Leviathan back into the deep. Ever since, a Titan Caller has stood watch in every age.
- **The crystals scattered.** The Sages planted a Lore Crystal on each island so knowledge could never be lost all at once again.
- **The Order of the Crystal Knights** was founded to guard the crystals. Its knights follow the **Crystal Code**: a long, very serious list of rules. It includes "Never leave a crystal behind" and "Always finish your vegetables."
- **Today.** Pirate ports, trading towns, jungle temples and crystal-powered airships. The Leviathan stirs again: it rises every few years and levels a city. The crystals are dimming faster than anyone can explain.

## Who's causing trouble

- **Captain Jumble**, a ghost-pirate sorcerer:
  - He failed the Sage exams three hundred years ago, by one spelling word. Since then he has sailed his ghost galleon, the *Misspelled*, around the isles scrambling words and numbers wherever he goes, for fun and for revenge.
  - He is loud, vain, theatrical and secretly insecure. He loves anagrams and hates being corrected.
  - His final defeat will be a Wit Duel.
- **The Iron Dominion**, an empire of masked commanders and drone fleets:
  - It wants to drain the Lore Crystals to control the Leviathan.
  - Its **scavenger drones** sniff out crystal shards wherever trouble breaks one.
- **Fiends** are creatures twisted by dimming crystals: Scrap Raptors, Volt Jellies, Magnet Beetles, Ink Slimes and worse. They burst into glowing motes when beaten.

## The party

Their voices and personalities are in `docs/game-design.md`.

- **Cade, the Crystal Knight:**
  - The newest knight of the Order, on his first real mission.
  - Earnest, formal and protective, and a terrible joke-teller. Quotes the Crystal Code constantly.
  - Fights with subtraction.
- **Kit:**
  - The Order's tutor droid, assigned to Cade.
  - Deadpan, dry and secretly fond of him. Kit's tiny pirate hat was won in a card game, and Kit insists it is intimidating.
  - Helps when the player is stuck.
- **Captain Wren, the Sky-Pirate Gunner:**
  - Captain of the airship *Brass Albatross*. Hired to fly Cade and a fresh Lore Crystal to Driftwood Isle.
  - Cocky, fast-talking, gives everything nicknames, and secretly the team's biggest cheerleader.
  - Fights with multiplication and division.
- **Knox, the Spellwright:**
  - A tiny, three-hundred-year-old scholar from the last Sage school. The K in Knox is silent, which is the Spellwright's favorite thing about it. Studied with Captain Jumble long ago, and passed the exam Jumble failed.
  - Speaks like an ancient wizard, then gets thrilled about silent letters.
  - Casts spells by spelling.
- **Maren, the Titan Caller:**
  - Keeper of the sea shrines. Her name means "of the sea". The Lore Crystals send her dreams when a Titan is needed.
  - Calm, wise, poetic, and grand when she summons.
  - Calls Titans with writing.

## Driftwood Isle (Chapter 1)

A small island of white beaches, jungle temples and a canyon of giant crystals. Its harbor town, **Driftwood Harbor**, is a pirate port built from shipwrecks. Its Lore Crystal has been flickering for weeks.

**Prologue: the crash.**
1. The *Brass Albatross* is carrying a fresh Lore Crystal to Driftwood Isle when Captain Jumble's ghost galleon rams it out of a cloud.
2. The Knight falls out over the beach at **Shipwreck Cove**, and Kit falls out over the Knight.
3. The airship crashes in the **Crystal Canyon**, and the new Lore Crystal shatters into **four shards**.
4. The crash wakes the **Geode Titan**, a colossal crystal-eating beast that has slept under the canyon since the Sundering.

**Places:**

| Place | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Shipwreck Cove** | Where the story starts. The hulk of a very old shipwreck (not theirs: "Ours is worse"), tide pools and a signpost Jumble scrambled. Scrap Raptors and Magnet Beetles scavenge the sand. |
| **The Temple Ruins** | A Sage temple swallowed by jungle, with glowing glyphs on the walls. Jumble has trapped the Spellwright here in a cage of scrambled words. Ink Slimes ooze around the steps. |
| **The Crystal Canyon** | Where the *Brass Albatross* came down. The Gunner is trying to repair her blasters. A crystal shrine is where the Titan Caller waits, and the Geode Titan's lair is deeper in. Dominion drones and Volt Jellies buzz around the wreck. |
| **Driftwood Harbor** (next) | The pirate port: Honest Hal's Previously Plundered Goods, the Salty Biscuit café (no grog, very good biscuits), the Sword Master's dock and the lighthouse with the island's Lore Crystal. |

**Running jokes on Driftwood Isle:**
- **Pockets, the three-eyed monkey,** turns up in every scene, and finding him is a collectible. He steals shiny things and loves squeaky ones. Captain Wren named him: everything shiny goes in his pockets, and he doesn't have pockets.
- **Jumble's scrambled signs:** "PELMET" for TEMPLE, "NYCOAN" for CANYON.
- **Kit's hat**, and the Knight's attempts at jokes.
